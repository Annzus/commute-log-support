01Local data

This policy describes Commute Log and the static support and privacy website. The app requires no separate app account, and its main features work offline.

Commute dates and statuses, daily adjustments, monthly cost settings and workday preferences are stored in a local database first. Reminder preferences, onboarding state and sync diagnostics also stay on the device. The iPhone app and widget share a database through an Apple App Group.

02Optional iCloud sync

When you enable sync, commute records, cost settings, workday preferences and technical sync metadata are sent to your Apple Account’s CloudKit private database. Metadata includes record, device actor and mutation identifiers and update times. A local scope identifier separates data belonging to different iCloud accounts.

The developer cannot view that private database in the CloudKit developer console. Apple processes iCloud data under its terms and privacy policy. Availability depends on account, network and storage conditions. Sync is not required to keep records locally.

03Permissions and sharing

The app includes no advertising, analytics SDK, location tracking or developer-operated backend. It does not request location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone or tracking permission. Notifications are optional, requested when enabling reminders and scheduled locally on the device.

Excel export opens the system share sheet. You decide whether and where to save or share the file. The destination’s rules apply once shared. The developer does not receive a copy through the export process.

04Retention and deletion

You can edit records and delete commute data in Settings. With sync enabled, deletions are queued for iCloud and your other devices. Delivery requires network access and an available iCloud account. Turning sync off does not delete local or remote data.

Uninstalling does not itself erase iCloud records, exported files or device backups. Those copies are governed by your chosen destination and Apple backup and iCloud settings. Shared container data may also remain while an extension stays installed.

Recovery after deletion cannot be promised. Shared files need to be removed from their destination separately. The developer cannot view or delete your private CloudKit records through the console on your behalf. Use the app’s data controls and Apple’s account settings.

05Support and this website

If you email support, the developer and email provider receive your address, message and attachments to handle your request and reply. Send only what is needed, never passwords, and redact unnecessary private records. Contact the address below if you would like support emails deleted.

These public pages use GitHub Pages static hosting. These pages load no external fonts, analytics scripts, ads or tracking pixels, set no analytics cookies and provide no online form collecting commute records.

Website hosting is separate from local app storage. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security when GitHub Pages is visited; its privacy statement governs that processing.
