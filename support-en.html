Common questions

A few helpful notes on recording days, estimating costs and keeping your data in sync.

How do I add or edit a commute?

Open Month and select a date to add or change its commute status. Use that day’s cost action to adjust a daily amount.

Choose your normal workdays in Settings. These days are used to calculate your commute rate.

How are costs calculated?

Costs are estimates, not bills from a transport provider. Configure fares, a monthly pass, gasoline or electric-car costs from the calendar’s cost action.

For fare, gasoline and EV models, a daily override replaces the default cost for that day. With a monthly pass, the daily amount is spending outside your pass and is added to the pass price. Check the month and currency before changing amounts.

What if iCloud is not syncing?

Sync is optional. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, have sync enabled in the app, and have access to iCloud, available storage and a working network.

Offline changes can sync when conditions allow. Turning sync off does not delete remote data. Keep the app and local records while troubleshooting instead of uninstalling. If the problem persists, send the sync status or error message to support. Never send your Apple Account password.

How do reminders and widgets work?

Choose a reminder time in app Settings and enable workday reminders. Allow notifications when prompted. If a reminder is missing, check iOS notification permissions, Focus and Scheduled Summary.

Add a widget from the iPhone Home Screen widget gallery. Widgets share the current day’s record with the app. iOS controls refresh timing, so a change may not appear immediately.

How do I export Excel?

Choose Excel export in app Settings, then use the system share sheet to save to Files or send to a destination you choose.

Exports may contain commute dates, statuses and costs. Choose a trusted destination. Deleting records in the app does not remove exported copies.

How do I delete my data?

Use the data deletion action in app Settings. Export first if you need a copy. Recovery after deletion cannot be promised.

With iCloud sync enabled, deletion changes are queued for the cloud and your other devices. Delivery requires a working network and iCloud account. Turning sync off or uninstalling does not erase cloud data. Read the privacy policy for details.
